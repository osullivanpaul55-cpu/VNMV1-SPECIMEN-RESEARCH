# Stage 4 — published Theraphosidae female morphometric contrast

This research pipeline compares three named adult-female tarantula exemplars across two genera, by sourcing individual voucher metrics rather than drawing universal family anatomy from one tarantula.

## Individually sourced females
- Aphonopelma eutylenum, female APH_1031: 15 published LEFT distal leg article lengths for I, III, IV; measured carapace length/width. Original study: Hamilton et al 2016, ZooKeys 560, doi:10.3897/zookeys.560.6264.
- Aphonopelma chalcodes, female APH_0887: 15 published LEFT distal article lengths for I, III, IV; carapace length/width. Same paper, *separate animal and species*.
- Chaetopelma persianum, female holotype ZMUT 2022: 20 published distal leg article lengths for I–IV, carapace length/width, and posterior spinneret segment measures. Zamani & West 2023, ZooKeys 1174, doi:10.3897/zookeys.1174.109135. The source gives a dorsal measurement method but does not name left/right side in the text used. Both sides in the GLB therefore remain illustrative.

The validated common spider segmentation is coxa, trochanter, femur, patella, tibia, metatarsus, tarsus. Distal article lengths vary across these vouchers. Femur-normalized ratios and leg totals divided by carapace length are **comparative observations, not family acceptance thresholds or evolutionary conclusions**.

## Review status and limitations
- Three neutral GLBs are *geometry-calibration props*: source-anchored lengths but **unmeasured coxa, trochanter, camera geometry, joint angles and foot contacts**; right-side values are unverified echoes of the source. The published values do not reconstruct a natural animal resting pose, male morphology or animation.
- A. chalcodes adult male APH_0954 has a published sex-specific source and carapace dimensions, but male segment series isn't transcribed or approved in this stage. Juvenile and spiderling morphometrics are unresolved. These three categories are held for future primary evidence.
- No real source photo bytes are used in mesh construction; no morphology is claimed validated from in-situ photographs.
- No transfer of APH_1031, APH_0887 or ZMUT lengths to Aphonopelma mooreae Card 0016 or the 587-card collection. The locked blue/copper appearance and provisional Card 0016 V3 geometry remain unchanged.
- No family-specific biomechanical joint constraints, skinned mesh, IK, occlusion-aware vision review, production animation, species master or human approval yet.

## Output and future five-runner integration
The Stage 4 workflow runs tests against three journal-sourced exemplar profiles and exports a comparison JSON with per-segment provenance, observed ratio spans, explicit unresolved areas, and three unapproved GLBs. It is a bounded scientific input to the existing five-worker taxonomy research; it does not automatically overwrite the nine generic family scaffolds.

After peer-reviewed measurements are gathered across more genera, sexes and life stages, propose genuinely different family templates; verify motion and body plan using actual source pixels and expert/human review, then adapt to distinct species in the private roster. Never silently imply 587 individually researched 3D specimens.
