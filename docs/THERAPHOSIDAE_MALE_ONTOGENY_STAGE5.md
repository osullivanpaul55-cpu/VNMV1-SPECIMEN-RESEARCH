# Stage 5 — mature male and juvenile-to-adult anatomy evidence

The public VNMV1 anatomical research runner now has TWO independently sourced adult male tarantula exemplars (35 femur-to-tarsus published article lengths) and a paired *Aphonopelma chalcodes* female comparison. These are samples of different individual spiders, not averages or a rule for sexual dimorphism.

## Male 1 — Aphonopelma chalcodes
- Male voucher APH_0954, Hamilton, Hendrixson & Bond 2016, ZooKeys 560, doi:10.3897/zookeys.560.6264, pp. 75–77: published left-leg I/III/IV distal segment measurements and retrolateral palpal tibia length.
- Source separately illustrates a palpal bulb/embolus and tibia-I mating clasper. Their shapes are **not** digitally reconstructed; lengths of many diagnostic surfaces are not published.
- Female voucher APH_0887 from prior Stage 4 provides 15 comparable left distal leg measurements. Ratios normalized to each carapace length distinguish those TWO INDIVIDUALS; no male/female species average can be inferred.

## Male 2 — Cymbiapophysa carmencita
- Male holotype ZFSQ-i11578, Peñaherrera-R. 2023, ZooKeys 1178, doi:10.3897/zookeys.1178.105703: Table 2 reports all four legs' femur, patella, tibia, metatarsus, tarsus, plus four palp articles, and the 10.38 x 8.59 mm carapace.
- Source describes **absence of tibial apophysis** for this species. The other male's tibial clasper must never be copied across species. Male palpal-bulb keels are described in the source but do not yield three-dimensional surface geometry.
- Published leg measurement side not independently resolved from the accessible table: source-side-unconfirmed evidence labels are retained, rather than claiming the left legs were measured.

## Ontogeny
- Schwerdt et al. 2021, Zoologischer Anzeiger 293, doi:10.1016/j.jcz.2021.05.005, follows *Grammostola vachoni* through growth and maturation; instar frequency and somatic developmental data are relevant to stage separation. The available abstract does not provide per-instar tarantula leg-article measurement series suitable for a 3D rig.
- Commission for Environmental Cooperation's tarantula growth guide illustrates *Brachypelma baumgarteni* 4th/6th/9th instars and adult female. Scale and appearance variation can be studied, not extrapolated to universal species proportions.
- Barrantes et al. 2026, doi:10.1002/jmor.70132, documents species-specific leg growth trajectories in **Theridiidae** (not tarantulas); it is used only as caution against uniform adult scaling.

## Generated data and hard boundaries
- 2 neutral, separately rooted mature-male GLB skeleton studies, 56 walking-leg segments each, **separate four-article male pedipalps**. No fabricated palpal bulb, embolus surface, or tibial spur mesh.
- 15 same-species observed male/female comparison rows, 12 six-angle evidence-review tasks (NOT 12 scientific 3D camera validations), and 3 juvenile/subadult source research holds. Camera projections not evaluated against authentic source photos.
- Coxa, trochanter, 3D joint orientations, right limb geometry and foot contacts remain **hypothetical**. A model cannot be called anatomically complete without the actual male palpal bulb or other verified diagnostics.
- Private Card 0016 *A. mooreae* skeleton V3 and locked blue-copper appearance remain untouched.
- No approved family rigs, male/female species masters, juvenile rigs or production-ready mobile animals. GitHub Actions outputs expire; archive evidence under the existing Drive research workspace.
